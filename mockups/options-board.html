The "Linear feel" is mostly engineering, not CSS. What you're responding to in Linear: optimistic writes (UI updates before the server answers), no spinners on warm paths, every surface reachable by keyboard, transform/opacity-only animation (never layout), and instant route changes from a local data cache. Diffect already has the right bones for this — local daemon, SSE, event log — so the latency budget is ours to keep. The M option just picks the transition grammar on top.
